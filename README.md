### 👋 About Me

I love computers and have explored a lot along the way — I started with **data analysis**, moved into **problem solving / competitive programming**, and eventually decided to go deeper into **full stack development**. Along that journey I picked up **Spring Boot** (building on my Java background), worked with **SQL databases** (from my data analysis days), and learned modern frontend tools like **JavaScript, React, Next.js, and Express**. I'm currently at a junior+ full stack level with a few real projects under my belt. Outside of code, I do **artwork** as a hobby 🎨.

- 🎓 Undergrad in **CSE** at **United International University (UIU)** — 3rd year running
- 💻 Full Stack Development (Java/Spring Boot + MERN)
- 🧩 Competitive Programming enthusiast
- 🎨 Artist in my free time
